1. Session expiry:
   10 minutes for an unjoined room/security code.
   Once joined, the communication remains active until
   disconnect, connection loss, or applicable inactivity timeout.

3. LAN IP:
   Do not hard-code one. Use localhost for development and
   environment variables for API/WebSocket URLs and CORS.
   Final deployment should use the public backend URL.

4. Security code:
   6-digit cryptographically random temporary code.
   Expires after 10 minutes. It is NOT the encryption key.

5. Identity:
   Default to anonymous "You" / "Partner".
   Optionally allow a temporary session-only display name.
   No permanent user-account system is required.

6. Branding:
   Use a clean cybersecurity-themed CipherLink UI.
   Dark navy/black + cyan/blue accents.
   No official MIT/MAHE branding until approved assets are provided.
   Keep all branding free.

7. Key authentication:
   Use X25519 + HKDF for key establishment and display
   SHA-256-based public-key fingerprints for manual verification.
   Do not claim X25519 alone authenticates identity.
   No paid certificate/identity infrastructure.

8. Test data:
   Use synthetic/public-domain images of approximately
   100 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 5 MB and 10 MB.
   Benchmark text sizes of 1 KB, 10 KB, 100 KB, 500 KB and 1 MB.
   Use these for encryption, latency, throughput, packet and
   image-security measurements.
